# Third-party components

This project interoperates with separately distributed FAST, Qwen3-VL, SigLIP, Gemma, V-JEPA2, PyTorch, Transformers, PEFT, NumPy, SciPy, Pillow, PyYAML, tokenizers, matplotlib, and optional Weights & Biases components. Their own licenses and terms apply.

The Physical Intelligence FAST tokenizer (`physical-intelligence/fast`) is distributed under Apache-2.0. Model weights are not redistributed by this repository. Obtain them from their official distribution channels and record the exact revision used.
